HEINEKEN introduces HEINEKEN Costa Rica, marking a new chapter in its long-term journey in the country and across Central America. It combines Costa Rica’s local strength, built over generations of Costa Rican talent, with HEINEKEN’s global scale, expertise, and portfolio. It positions Costa Rica as a strategic regional hub to accelerate growth, innovation, and commercial execution.
