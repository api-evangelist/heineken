As World Water Day approaches, it provides an opportunity to reflect on the importance of water and to raise awareness on global water challenges. The UN has defined the theme for this year being ‘Water and Gender’. While water challenges affect everyone, they don’t affect all in the same way; especially in places where access to safe drinking water is limited.
