With the second episode of Praise the Bar , HEINEKEN’s YouTube series celebrating bar culture and togetherness, the spotlight turns to Amsterdam and to one of the city’s most beloved Brown Cafés : Café Papeneiland . Tucked away in the Jordaan, Papeneiland is more than a bar. It is a living room for locals, a meeting place for neighbours, and a reminder of why bars matter, not just as places to drink, but as spaces where human connection thrives.
