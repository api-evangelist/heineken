The latest episode of Praise the Bar takes viewers to Seville, Spain, where bars are more than places to eat and drink ; they are spaces where traditions are preserved, communities come together, and future hospitality talent is nurtured . Based in the heart of the Andalusian capital, Taberna Zurbarán embodies the spirit of Sevillian hospitality, showing how bars continue to play a vital role in everyday life. The episode also highlights the work of Fundación Cruzcampo in helping develop the next generation of hospitality professionals.
