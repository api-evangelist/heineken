Growth: Global and local power brands in focus markets driving growth Total volume increased 1.6%, accelerating in Q2; consolidated volume grew 0.4%, and licensed volume up 23.2%. All five global brands delivered growth, with Heineken® volume up 5.3% and Tiger returning to volume growth. Priority segments delivered superior volume growth: premium grew 6%, beyond beer up 8%, LoNo 12% higher.
